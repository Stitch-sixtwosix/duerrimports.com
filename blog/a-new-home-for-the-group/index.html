A new home for the group

Duerr Imports has a new website. Here is what the group does today and where each company fits.

November 4, 2026 · Duerr Imports · 1 min read

Duerr Imports started as an import house. Today it is a US holding group with six operating businesses alongside the import side, and this site is now where all of them meet.

Technology

  • Proplexa builds property management software for owners and managers. proplexa.ai
  • HostFlow is short-term rental management from Proplexa and Medellín Concierge. hostflow.in
  • EminentTracker gives eminent domain attorneys the parcels in the path of planned public projects, and who owns them, years before the notice. eminenttracker.com
  • LeadGenAI builds B2B lead systems for niche markets: a market map, a buying-signal engine, outreach and reporting. leadgenai.io

Real estate

  • FastFlip Realty helps owners sell a property quickly and simply. fastflip.co
  • Medellín Concierge handles rentals, real estate and property management in Medellín. medellinconcierge.net

Imports

The import business still runs wholesale and white-label importing for cafés, offices and resellers. If you want a product sourced abroad and brought to the US, get in touch.
