Introducing HostFlow

Proplexa and Medellín Concierge have built HostFlow, one place to run short-term rental properties.

November 3, 2026 · Duerr Imports · 1 min read

Running short-term rentals means juggling pricing, owners, cleaners and paperwork across too many tools. HostFlow brings that work into one place.

HostFlow is a product by Proplexa, the group's property management software company, and Medellín Concierge, which manages rentals and property on the ground in Medellín.

What it covers

  • Revenue automation to keep pricing and income on track.
  • Owner portals so property owners can see what is happening with their homes.
  • Cleaner scheduling between guest stays.
  • Compliance, kept alongside everything else rather than in a separate system.

Who it is for

Hosts and property managers who run short-term rentals for themselves or for owners. HostFlow is available in English and Spanish.

Try it

Visit hostflow.in to sign in or set up a new organization.
