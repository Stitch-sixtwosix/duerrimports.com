Businesses come to Duerr Imports for one of two things: buying imported products in quantity, or selling imported products under their own name. They sound alike, but they suit different goals.

## Wholesale

You buy existing products by the case at trade prices, then use or resell them as they are.

Wholesale suits you if:

- you run a café, office or shop and need regular stock,
- the product's own brand is fine for your customers,
- you want to start quickly with no design work.

## White label

We source a product abroad and it arrives with **your** brand on it, labelled and shipped for you.

White label suits you if:

- you want your own product line without manufacturing it yourself,
- your brand is what customers buy from you,
- you plan to sell the product over the long term.

## What to send us

Whichever you choose, the quickest start is an email to [contact@duerrimports.com](mailto:contact@duerrimports.com) with:

1. your business name,
2. the product or type of product you want,
3. roughly how many units you need.

We will reply with the next steps.
